# Graffiti.pc #189: active-root order and far-singleton transfer

Strict Lean partials under the exact even-distance cap, with connectivity at least d−2. The complete conjecture remains open.

* [ActiveStarHeight189](src/ActiveStarHeight189.lean): At d≥6, an active omitted star with s≤2 pins has at most d+s+1 vertices in any counterexample. The star order plus pin count equals the root degree plus one. Hence a normalized high omitted attachment has degree in [d,d+2s]. This applies with no fourth-shell hypothesis, including the separated-double and zero-fourth singleton regimes. A larger star would force radius two from a high flank in the complementary shore: the would-be far set would lie behind too few cut ports and remaining distance-two vertices. Bounding the order by the same charged sets then contradicts the earlier lower floor.
* [FarTriangleTransfer189](src/FarTriangleTransfer189.lean): In the residual pin triangle with a fourth-shell vertex, write h=d+δ for the omitted star order, δ=0 or 1. If d+3δ≥11, the same normalized longest path has a high omitted vertex in this star with two separated pins. This covers d≥11 for h=d and d≥8 for h=d+1. It redirects the far-singleton case to the separated-double case, not a proof of traceability by itself. This uses a three-way transfer: partition the star according to adjacency to the pin, charge the two regional budgets, then exact Hall distinctness forces a high matched star neighbor adjacent to two path ports. The auxiliary Python check covers the arithmetic constraints only; it is not a graph search.
