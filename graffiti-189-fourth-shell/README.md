# Graffiti.pc #189: fourth-shell reciprocity and bounded omitted stars

Strict Lean partial, with no upper-order restriction in the hypotheses. The full conjecture remains open.

- [FarReciprocity189](src/FarReciprocity189.lean): If x has just one fourth-shell vertex w, then w has at most two fourth-shell vertices; every neighbor of w is at distance three from x, and all but at most one neighbor of x are at distance three from w. If w is itself an omitted high-degree attachment on the same normalized path, its only fourth-shell vertex is x, it has one pin, and its exact-size star boundary has rigid flanks. The [clique-layer fixture](far_fixture.py) checks that two fourth-shell vertices at w are possible without that longest-path premise. The fixture is traceable, not a counterexample to #189.
- [StarSize189](src/StarSize189.lean): Every active omitted star has at most 2d vertices under the distance-two cap. For a path pin t of root x, the pin's flank f is outside the star boundary. Star vertices adjacent to t are at distance two from f, and all the others are at distance two from t via x. This identifies an important scope issue with the earlier conditional large-star pin-gap application: its hypothesis of star size at least 2d+3 cannot occur for an active omitted star. The general universal-terminal-path theorem and the earlier conditional statements remain correct; no source was removed.
- [ExactStarOpposite189](src/ExactStarOpposite189.lean): For d≥6, any active high-degree omitted root with at most two pins and an exact-size cut of d−2 cannot have a complementary component of size at most ceil(d/2) in a counterexample. The revised proof uses the uniform star bound above and no hypothetical large-star branch. This includes the singleton fourth-shell branch and strengthens the previous separated-pair restriction.
- [LitBoundary189](src/LitBoundary189.lean): For a singleton fourth-shell root, let m count cut ports other than its pin t that are not adjacent to t. Its order is at most 3d+1+md: every otherwise uncounted vertex must lie one edge beyond one of these m ports, each of which can reach at most d complementary-shore vertices by parity charging. This excludes m=0 for d≥7 and m≤1 for d≥10 by the previous order floor.
