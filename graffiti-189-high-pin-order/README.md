# Graffiti.pc #189: the first-slack order floor

**Lean-checked partial results.** With degree threshold \(d\ge6\), at most one vertex of degree below \(d\), at most \(d+1\) vertices at even distance from any vertex (including itself), and connectivity at least \(d-2\), a nontraceable graph has at least \(4d-4\) vertices. The analogous bound for \(d=5\) is \(4d-6\). In particular this proves the catalogue claim in this connectivity range for all orders strictly below the stated floors. Larger orders and lower connectivity remain open.

* [Normalized attachment dichotomy](src/HighDichotomy189.lean). A nonspanning longest path has a high-degree omitted attachment: a sole omitted vertex was already excluded by the old omitted-edge tie breaker, and otherwise a unique low-degree attachment would be a cut vertex. The edge-minimal normalization from the earlier pin-budget package leaves either one pin with at most one distance-four vertex, or two separated pins with no distance-four vertices and a size-\(d-2\) boundary cut.
* [Stronger gap exchange](src/GapStrong189.lean). The sum of the porter and midpoint degrees is at most \(d+4\); if the midpoint has any omitted neighbor, the bound improves to \(d+3\). Indeed at equality the two off-path neighbor groups exhaust the distance-two sphere of the right flank. The flank then must be adjacent to every other vertex of the connected path support, and rotation produces a midpoint endpoint with an omitted neighbor, allowing extension. For \(d\ge6\), this eliminates gap-two pairs even for the unique low-degree porter in a nontrivial omitted component.
* [Interior-hole lemma](src/Interior189.lean) and [order floor](src/MarkedOrder189.lean). An interior pin cannot see both path endpoints: its porter would need to spend too many slots, counting its existing flanks and the two endpoint vertices. This leaves a different unused endpoint-count index for each interior pin. A reachable omitted component has at least \(d-2\) pins and \(d-1\) vertices, and rotating to high-degree endpoints preserves the edge-minimal support. Counting the endpoint marks, the holes and the component yields the order floor.
