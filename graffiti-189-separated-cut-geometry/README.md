# Graffiti.pc #189: matching ports of a saturated star

**Strict partial, unrestricted order.** At threshold d, an omitted high-degree vertex with two separated pins on a longest path and an omitted neighbor has a saturated star boundary of size k=d−2 when the graph has connectivity at least k and meets the even-distance cap. These modules establish additional structure of that boundary. They do not settle #189.

* [PortHall189](src/PortHall189.lean): In any exact size-k vertex cut, each shore of size at least k admits distinct neighbors for all cut vertices simultaneously. If a set of ports failed Hall, deleting the other ports and the union of its shore neighbors would separate the remaining shore with fewer than k vertices. The theorem applies to the saturated omitted star, yielding distinct omitted-star neighbors for all d−2 boundary vertices.
* [DenseStar189](src/DenseStar189.lean): Under a global distance-two bound d, a set of size h≥2d+3 centered at a universal vertex is Hamilton-connected in its induced graph. Its minimum internal degree is at least h−d−1, since every internal nonneighbor is at distance two. Consequently any two saturated boundary vertices represented on the chosen longest path are separated by at least h+1 positions when the star is this large. Distinct matched neighbors and a prescribed-terminal spanning path through the star give the forbidden splice for any shorter gap.

Lean and Mathlib 4.19.0. Compile the previous first-slack package, including WideWing189, and ensure Mathlib.Combinatorics.Hall.Finite is built. Add prerequisites to the Mathlib LEAN_PATH and run replay.sh. Builds use warnings as errors and a fresh object directory.

## Complementary-shore parity

* [ForeignPorts189](src/ForeignPorts189.lean): A vertex across an exact cut pays one distinct distance-two slot per adjacent matched port. The stronger inequality counts every neighbor of its adjacent ports on the other shore, plus every distance-two vertex outside that shore. Tight Hall sets can be exchanged with their shore-neighbor set to produce another exact separator.
* If a complementary vertex sees q ports with 2q>d, the leftover distance-two budget and missing ports cannot separate any vertex of that shore at distance at least three. This vertex has distance at most two to the entire complementary shore and at most d−q nonneighbors there.
* [TruncatedHall189](src/TruncatedHall189.lean): A port set of size q has at least min(q,h) neighbors in any size-h shore, even if h is smaller than the exact cut. Therefore a vertex adjacent to q ports with 2q>d spends at least q distance-two slots into the large star shore and at least min(q,h) slots into any additional separate shore, forcing h+q≤d.
* [SmallOpposite189](src/SmallOpposite189.lean): At d≥7, a separated-double-pin counterexample cannot have any complementary component of order at most d/2. A high-degree vertex of such a component sees more than d/2 cut ports; the singleton exception has the same property by connectivity. Charging ports into the star and the remaining complementary components forces the whole complement to be small. If the star is large, the matched spanning splice is longer than the available path interval; otherwise the total order contradicts the preceding lower floor. Thus every complementary component of a counterexample in this branch must have more than d/2 vertices. There is no upper-order or connectivity assumption on the complementary components themselves.

The final module also requires UltraOrder189 from the preceding one-exception terminal routing package.
