# Graffiti.pc #384b with two degree-two vertices sharing a neighbor

**Let G be a finite simple graph with 29 distinct vertex triangle counts and exactly 27 vertices of degree at least three. If two distinct degree-two vertices have a common neighbor, then γ₂(G) ≤ |V(G)| − 15.** The two degree-two vertices may have further adjacencies consistent with these hypotheses. The theorem applies to disconnected graphs as well. In fact `shared_anchor` replaces the assumption on the number of high vertices by the condition that at least 29 vertices have degree at least two. [Graph-level theorems](SharedAnchorClose.lean)

The original conjecture was generated by Ermelinda DeLaViña’s Graffiti.pc. The triangle count at a vertex is the number of unordered triangles containing it. Every vertex outside a 2-dominating set must have two distinct neighbors in it. With 29 count values, the conjectured bound asks for 15 omissions. The frozen low-mate result shows that a failure in the two- or three-ear cases has pairwise nonadjacent degree-two vertices, which we call ears, with all neighbors high. A family of d ears in such a failure uses at least 2d − 1 distinct neighbors. [Official catalogue](https://www.uhd.edu/academics/sciences/mathematics-statistics/wowii/index.html) · [Pinned low-mate predecessor](https://github.com/vjdad4m/maths/tree/a67903e95adbeb29cace2474c9b2e59efba6058a/graffiti-384b-low-mate)

## Why three anchors suffice

1. Two ears with a common neighbor have exactly three neighbors between them in a hypothetical failure. Reserve these three high vertices as anchors. The other 24 high vertices form the pool X. Both ears are available for omission throughout. A five-clique in X yields a cut with one pivot counted on both sides. The omissions on the two sides then total at least 24 + 1 + 2·2 = 29, so one side reaches 15. The pinned cut and weighted cubic-neighbor argument implement this step even when deletion of the pivot lowers a cubic degree. Consequently X contains no five-clique in a failure. [Cut with exterior omissions](SharedCut.lean) · [Cubic-neighbor count](SharedFive.lean)
2. Let m count the pool vertices with any neighbor outside X. At most two ears meet any fixed anchor w. Indeed, for a family of d ears meeting w there are at most d + 1 neighbors and the earlier bound gives 2d − 1 ≤ d + 1. Any triangle at w whose other two vertices are high uses two of the other anchors or exposed pool vertices. A low vertex on a triangle is an ear and supports only one triangle. Hence every anchor score is at most C(m + 2, 2) + 2. The 27 high scores are distinct and all at least two. The three reserved scores force the pool score at zero-based rank i to be at least i + 5 once C(m + 2, 2) + 2 ≤ i + 4. [Anchor cap and rank gap](AnchorScores.lean)
3. Sample nine vertices of X and initially keep every vertex outside X. Remove both exterior ears, and remove sampled vertices with clique neighborhoods whenever they have two retained neighbors. Counting each excess repair over all C(24,9) = 1,307,504 samples gives the contradiction. A vertex with no exterior contact uses the tripartite Turán degree bound inside X. A vertex with a contact needs only one further retained neighbor. The computation uses the three score gaps for m ≤ 5 and the number of exposed vertices alone for m ≥ 6. [Two-ear sampling](SharedTrial.lean) · [Rank accounting](SharedBudget.lean)

## Scope and reuse

For a remaining failure with at least 29 nonleaves, `residual_distinct` gives exactly 27 high vertices. At 29 nonleaves the two ears use four distinct high neighbors. At 30 nonleaves the three ears use six distinct high neighbors. These disjoint-neighborhood regimes remain open in this package. In particular, reserving all four anchors for two disjoint ears leaves only 23 high vertices in X. The same single-pivot count would give 23 + 1 + 4 = 28 omissions across both sides, compatible with fourteen on each side. The separate lower nonleaf quota is outside this result. [Residual theorem](SharedAnchorClose.lean)

The cut and cubic count adapt `PendantClose.paired` and `PendantClose.five_free` to a finite set of exterior omissions. The sampling proof reuses `PendantRank.clean`, its retained-neighbor deletion lemma, and the clique-neighborhood activation count, now on 24 vertices. The proof imports the earlier local Turán estimate, the boundary fiber count, and the rank and maximal-diversity lemmas. The structural additions are the common bound on the three reserved scores, the resulting rank jump, and the seven-case repair comparison.
