# Graffiti.pc #384b for 31 nonleaves and 31 triangle counts

**Every finite simple graph with exactly 31 vertices of degree at least two and exactly 31 distinct vertex triangle counts satisfies γ₂(G) ≤ |V(G)| − 16.** The theorem allows disconnected graphs and every graph order. It covers the requested ten-contact case and all of the previously remaining range through fourteen. The full catalogue conjecture includes other profiles. [Catalogue theorem and numerical specialization](UniformCuts.lean) · [Ermelinda DeLaVina’s Graffiti.pc catalogue](https://www.uhd.edu/academics/sciences/mathematics-statistics/wowii/index.html)

Here γ₂ is the minimum size of a vertex set giving every vertex outside it at least two neighbors in the set. The triangle count of a vertex counts unordered triangles through it, and diversity counts the distinct values over all vertices. Both definitions and the catalogue bound are imported from the existing #384b proof. [Shared research index](https://github.com/vjdad4m/maths/blob/main/README.md) · [Predecessor package](../graffiti-384b-nine-contacts/)

## Proof

1. In a hypothetical failure there are two degree-two vertices, called ears. Their four neighbors are distinct and have degree at least three. Call these neighbors the anchors. The other 25 vertices of degree at least three form the exterior. Among all 29 high vertices the triangle counts are distinct and at least two. Earlier theorems give at most fourteen exterior contacts and an anchor w whose exterior neighbors are all private to w and induce a matching with possible isolated vertices.
2. **Every other anchor has triangle count at least ten.** Write x for the number of exterior neighbors of w. Its triangle count is at most 2 + ⌊x/2⌋. The frozen sampling estimate assigns w a saving of at least 6,904,448. Count two uses the earlier ear saving. At larger counts, x ≤ 7 gives a rank at most three and degree at least five, and x ≥ 8 gives a rank at most seven and degree at least nine. A second anchor of count at most nine would cost at least 4,576,264, and the last two each cost at least 739,024. The total 12,958,760 exceeds the available 12,407,000. [Rank and degree proof](TenGap.lean)
3. The root w has a triangle whose other two vertices are high. Indeed, w touches only one ear, and that ear can lie on only one triangle through w. The other two high vertices are either both anchors or both exterior. A mixed triangle would give an exterior neighbor of w a second anchor neighbor. [Triangle selection and final assembly](UniformCuts.lean)
4. **Triangle among the anchors.** Put the ears on opposite sides of a cut, each opposite its own anchors. The three anchors forming the triangle are already supported by two opposite vertices. Let b be the fourth anchor. An edge to the other ear’s anchors completes the cut. So does an exterior neighbor shared with b’s mate, or a three-vertex path in b’s exterior neighborhood, using a four-cycle through b. In the absence of all three configurations the exterior neighbors induce a matching and there are no mixed triangles at b. Its triangle count is at most 2 + ⌊14/2⌋ = 9, contradicting the score gap. [Opposite-ear cut](OppositeFan.lean)
5. **Triangle through private exterior neighbors.** Put both ears opposite all four anchors. An exterior vertex touching two anchors can join the ears. At each other anchor still lacking support, a three-vertex path in its private neighborhood supplies a four-cycle. Such a path must exist by the score gap and the same matching estimate. These paths belong to disjoint private neighborhoods and can be added simultaneously. Now every fixed vertex except w has two opposite neighbors. [Simultaneous four-cycles](ForkTip.lean)
6. Use the two parities available through the private triangle at w. The frozen flexible-region theorem either completes the cut or isolates an exterior region C from all fixed vertices except w. On C, at most one triangle through any vertex can use w because w’s exterior neighborhood induces a matching. Every vertex of C with internal degree at most two must therefore have original triangle count exactly two, be adjacent to w, and have positive internal degree. The distinct high counts make this exceptional vertex unique if it exists. Take a maximum cut of C. Every other vertex has at least two opposite neighbors, and the exceptional vertex has at least one. Orient C so the exceptional vertex lies opposite w. Their joining edge supplies the second opposite neighbor at both ends. When all internal degrees are at least three, either endpoint of the private edge can serve as the joining vertex. The completed cut protects both ears, contradicting the earlier two-ear frame theorem. [Joining two pending vertices](JoiningTips.lean) · [Closed-region proof](ExteriorCut.lean)

## Reuse and replay

The argument uses the checked 31/31 profile and disjoint-anchor theorems, the link estimates, the eleven-vertex rank and degree account, and the two-ear cut obstruction. The simultaneous four-cycle construction adapts ForkGuard34.matching while reserving w’s exterior neighbors. The two-parity extension is the verified FlexibleTip theorem. The new joining argument allows one vertex of deficient internal degree and allows w to have more than two exterior neighbors. The final theorem has no private-pair or numerical contact assumption. Build the [predecessor package](../graffiti-384b-nine-contacts/) and its documented source dependencies. · [Adapted cut construction](ForkTip.lean)

Use Lean and Mathlib v4.19.0 (Lean commit `6caaee842e94`, Mathlib commit `c44e0c8ee63ca166450922a373c7409c5d26b00b`). Build the nine-contact package and retain its predecessor modules on the Lean search path. Also include the Lean source or build directory for the imported #384b modules on that path as needed. Compile the six new modules in `modules.txt` order with synchronous elaboration and warnings as errors. For example, with the dependencies on `LEAN_PATH` and Lean launched from a pinned Mathlib environment, prepend the writable extension build directory to `LEAN_PATH` and run:

```sh
sha256sum -c NEW-SOURCES.sha256
mkdir -p build
export LEAN_PATH="$PWD/build:${LEAN_PATH:-}"
while read -r module; do
  lean -DwarningAsError=true -DElab.async=false -o "build/$module.olean" "$module.lean" \
    > "$module.log" 2>&1 || { cat "$module.log"; exit 1; }
done < modules.txt
python3 audit.py .
```
